Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes the intended handling of information by Topps Checklist Generator, a personal trading card checklist automation project currently under development.

Information and access

The application is intended for use by its owner. If the owner authorizes Google Drive access, Google provides credentials that permit the application to create and manage files it creates or opens with the app. The application requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope, not unrestricted Google Drive access.

How information is used

Google authorization credentials are used solely to authenticate the application to Google Drive. Google Drive access is used to create folders, upload generated checklist PDFs, and update those files when the checklist information changes. Contact email information may be used to send operational notifications if that feature is configured.

Storage and sharing

Generated PDFs are intended to be stored in the account owner's Google Drive. Application credentials should be stored in private configuration or secret-management systems, not on this public website. The project does not intend to sell personal information or share Google user data with advertisers.

Retention and control

The account owner can delete generated files from Google Drive and revoke the application's access through their Google Account security settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized Drive operations until access is granted again. Files already created may remain in Drive until deleted.

Security

The project is designed to use Google's OAuth authorization and limited Drive permissions. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the project develops or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.
