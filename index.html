Topps Checklist Generator

Topps Checklist Generator is a personal automation project designed to organize publicly released trading card checklists into printable PDF documents. Its intended coverage includes baseball, basketball, football, and Star Wars trading card releases.

How Google Drive is used

With the account owner's authorization, the application uses Google Drive to create and update its generated checklist PDF files and related folders. It requests the limited drive.file permission, rather than full access to the account's Drive.

This project is under development. This website is informational and does not offer public sign-up or a hosted checklist-generation service.
